Random space facts

A little collection of surprising facts about the planets, stars, and worlds around us.

01

A day on Venus lasts about 243 Earth days—longer than its year, which is about 225 Earth days.

02

The Moon is slowly moving away from Earth at about 3.8 centimeters per year.

03

Jupiter’s Great Red Spot is a giant storm that has been observed for centuries.

04

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to travel the 150 million kilometers to Earth.

05

Saturn’s average density is lower than water’s, though there is no ocean large enough to float it in.

06

Mars is home to Olympus Mons, a shield volcano about 22 kilometers high.

07

Neptune’s winds can reach speeds of more than 2,000 kilometers per hour.

08

The Sun contains more than 99% of the total mass of our solar system.

09

A teaspoon of neutron-star material would have an enormous mass—roughly a billion tons on Earth.
